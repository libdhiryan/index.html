About the founder

Hi, I'm Liberty.

For nine-plus years I've lived inside B2B sales, business development, and client relationship management — the discovery calls, the proposal-stage haggling, the CFO who wants ROI before quarter-end. I've felt what it's like to lose a deal to an objection nobody caught in time.

CloserAI is the debrief partner I wished I'd had the whole way through: something that listens back to every call so you don't have to guess what went right, what went missing, and what to send next.

"My vision is simple — every sales conversation deserves a second listen. CloserAI is that second listen, working quietly in the background so your team can focus on the conversation itself."
— Liberty
